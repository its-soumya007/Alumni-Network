About SVU Alumni Connect

Connecting past, present, and future students of Swami Vivekananda University.

Our Mission

SVU Alumni Connect is an exclusive digital platform designed to bridge the gap between alumni and current students. Whether you are seeking career guidance, looking to refer a talented junior, or just want to catch up with old classmates, this portal provides a secure and intuitive environment to build valuable professional connections.

Platform Features

  • Community Feed: Explore and filter through detailed reference opportunities and company reviews posted directly by SVU alumni.
  • Real-Time 1-to-1 Chat: Instantly connect with mentors, peers, and alumni with our WhatsApp-style fast messaging system.
  • Alumni Directory & Search: Quickly find alumni profiles, complete with their current company and professional designation.
  • Interactive Engagement: Like posts, view detailed image attachments, and track your most recent conversations seamlessly.
